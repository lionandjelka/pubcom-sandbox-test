## Discussion prompts

**Access:** Did the author have write/branch permissions? Did Handler have permission to assign reviewers/apply labels? Could invited reviewers comment and resolve threads?  
**Traceability:** Could everyone locate the latest PDF, summary, manuscript version, reviewer concerns, Handler recommendation and Chair decision?  
**Policy:** Were category, stream, timelines, authorship/attribution and data rights unambiguous? What requires comparison to the approved current manual?  
**Interface:** Did GitHub's platform `Approve`, `Request changes` or `Resolve conversation` labels confuse the scientific meaning?  
**Next pilot:** What should be changed before inviting a real manuscript? Who will own each change?
